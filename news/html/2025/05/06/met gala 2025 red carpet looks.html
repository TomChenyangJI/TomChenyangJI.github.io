2025 Met Gala: Blue Carpet Roundup

See the looks from fashion’s biggest night. Choose, save and share six of your favorite images.

Rihanna in Marc Jacobs.

By The Styles DeskPhotographs by Amir Hamja and Nina WesterveltVideo by Chevaz Clarke-Williams

Updated May 5, 2025 at 11:45 p.m. E.T.

And just like that… a few minutes after 10 p.m., Rihanna appeared. Showing off her bare legs covered by a skirt-suit designed by Marc Jacobs, the pop star turned mogul also took the opportunity to show off her baby bump.

Although New York City may have been a bit damp and dreary, the weather didn’t deter celebrities from every corner of the cultural spectrum from showing off their wares at the 2025 Met Gala. Rihanna capped a lively procession of celebrities who walked down the blue carpet, including Madonna, Stevie Wonder and Andre 3000, the latter wearing piano as backpack.

Celebrating the Metropolitan Museum of Art’s Costume Institute show “Superfine: Tailoring Black Style,” host Anna Wintour, the chief content officer of Condé Nast and the editor in chief of Vogue magazine, were joined by co-chairs ASAP Rocky, Lewis Hamilton, Colman Domingo and Pharrell Williams — honorary chair LeBron James announced earlier in the day that he wouldn’t join due to a knee injury, but his wife, Savannah, would still be attending. The show marks a first for the Costume Institute, in that it focuses on designers of color and is the first in two decades dedicated to men’s wear.

But the interpretation of the theme was up for grabs, as you’ll see below, where you’re also able to choose and share some of your favorite looks from the evening.
